Why a partner

People who commit to a specific person are up to 95% more likely to hit their goal. Not an app. Not a streak counter. A human who notices when you skip.

01 / 04

Tell us what you're chasing.

Pick a goal, how often you want to check in, and whether you want a gentle cheerleader or a tough coach.

02 / 04

We find your counterpart.

Our matching weighs goal overlap, timezone, schedule and communication style. Most people are matched in under 48 hours.

03 / 04

Meet, then commit.

A 15-minute intro call. If it clicks, you both sign a lightweight pact: what you'll do, and when you'll report back.

04 / 04

Show up for each other.

Weekly check-ins, a shared streak, and nudges when one of you goes quiet. Rematch any time, no questions.

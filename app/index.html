Tandem

One person, one goal, regular check-ins.

Tandem is built for ADHD brains. You're paired with one partner who has ADHD too, so you get someone who understands the Monday-motivation, Thursday-disappeared pattern. Small steps, forgiving streaks, and company when you're stuck.
